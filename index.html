Features

Built for quick, thrilling missions

Short to play, hard to put down. Here is what is waiting in the cockpit.

38 missions

A 30-mission campaign plus 8 bonus missions with their own unlock goals.

8 themed worlds

From desert dunes and open ocean to night operations, snowstorms and a burning final battlefield.

Boss battles

Face giant boss aircraft with multiple attack phases, escort fighters and missile salvos.

Varied objectives

Dogfights, ground strikes, escorts, base defense, checkpoint runs, coin rushes, missile dodging and survival.

6 fighter jets

Start in the F-1 Falcon and unlock faster, tougher fighters up to the gold Phoenix SE. Each one handles differently.

Upgrades

Spend coins on engine, armor, guns and missiles, five levels each.

Earn 3 stars

Finish fast, keep your hull intact and hit your shots to earn stars, milestones and coin rewards.

Play your way

Easy, Normal or Hard. Floating or fixed joystick, optional tilt steering, radar, lock-on missiles and daily rewards.

The final battlefield

Chase down the giants

Campaign bosses fight back in phases, with escorts and missile salvos. Survive them, and the burning final battlefield is yours.

About the game

Arcade flying, made for your pocket

Skyfang is a single-player arcade air combat game made for quick sessions. Each mission takes a few minutes and has a clear goal, so you can jump in, fly hard and come back for one more.

Work through the campaign world by world, earn stars and coins, unlock new aircraft in the Hangar and tune them in the Upgrade Bay. Pick Easy to learn the ropes or Hard when you want a real fight.

  • Desert dunes
  • Green valleys
  • Open ocean
  • Mountain passes
  • Night operations
  • Snowstorms
  • Enemy fortresses
  • Final battlefield
