Skyfang: Airplane Games

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026 · Applies to the Skyfang: Airplane Games app for iOS and Android

This policy explains what information is processed when you play Skyfang: Airplane Games (the "App"), why, and the choices you have. The App is published by Abdur ("we", "us").

In short: we do not ask for your name, email, location or any account, and the App has no sign-in, chat or in-app purchases. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The App shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device and advertising data to serve and measure those ads, and in the EEA, UK and Switzerland it only uses that data for personalized ads if you consent.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game progress and settings in a file on your device: coins, experience and level, unlocked aircraft and upgrades, mission stars and progress, daily-reward timing, and settings such as sound volume, controls, difficulty and language. This file:

  • is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it;
  • contains no personal information;
  • is deleted when you uninstall the App, or reset with Settings → Reset progress in the App.

2. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, receive or store personal data from the App. The App does not use Unity Analytics, crash reporting or any other analytics service, and it does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads: banner ads on menu screens, occasional full-screen ads between missions, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch (for example, to continue a mission or double your coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is built into the App. To serve, measure and protect ads, Google collects and processes information such as:

  • Device identifiers: on Android the Advertising ID and App Set ID; on iOS the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only if you allow tracking, see below) and the Identifier for Vendors;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (for example, country or city);
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, screen size, app version and network type;
  • Ad interaction data, such as ads shown, taps and whether a rewarded video was completed;
  • Diagnostic data about the ad SDK's performance and errors.

Google uses this information to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, show personalized ads based on your interests. Google acts as an independent controller of this data under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information in advertising
  • Data disclosures for the Google Mobile Ads SDK: Android · iOS

Ads are filtered so that content stays suitable for a teen audience. The App does not sell your information, and we do not receive any information from Google that identifies you personally.

4. Your privacy choices

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are personalized. You can accept, reject or choose specific purposes. If you do not consent, Google may still show ads that are not based on your personal profile, using limited data as permitted by law. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Other regions

Where privacy laws in your region (for example, some US states) give you the right to opt out of targeted advertising, a privacy message may be shown, and you can also use the device settings below.

iPhone and iPad: App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, the App asks for your permission before your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used to track you across apps. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings, usually under Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • On your device: game progress stays until you reset it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage or delete Google activity data at myaccount.google.com, and reset your device's advertising identifier as described above.
  • Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to delete. You can still contact us with any request and we will respond and help you exercise your rights with Google.

8. Security

Your saved game is stored locally in the App's private storage and protected against corruption. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

9. International data transfers

Google may process advertising data in the United States and other countries where it or its service providers operate. Google relies on legal safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for these transfers.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. You can withdraw ad consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. For data held by Google, see the links in section 3. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the App changes, for example if a new service is added. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
